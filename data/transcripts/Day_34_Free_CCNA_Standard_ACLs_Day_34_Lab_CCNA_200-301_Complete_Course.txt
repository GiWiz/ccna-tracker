Standard ACLs can be used to  identify traffic based on the source IP address of the packet, and then either permit or deny  it. There are two main ways to configure them, standard numbered ACLs and standard named  ACLs. In this lab, we’ll practice both. So let’s get right into it. NETWORK 203.0.113.0 0.0.0.3 AREA 0. Let’s see if it was enabled on  each interface. DO SHOW IP OSPF INTERFACE.
