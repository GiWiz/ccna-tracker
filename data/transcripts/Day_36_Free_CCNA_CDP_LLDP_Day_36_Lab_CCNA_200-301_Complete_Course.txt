LLDP TRANSMIT. LLDP RECEIVE. Okay, finally SW1 and R1, SW1 first. EXIT. NO CDP RUN. LLDP RUN. INTERFACE G0/1.  LLDP TRANSMIT. LLDP RECEIVE. And last, R1. CONF T. NO CDP RUN. LLDP RUN.
